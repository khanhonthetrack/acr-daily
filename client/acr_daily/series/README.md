# The ranked series' logos

Drawn for ACR Daily (tools/series_logos: logos.html, rendered by render.js): original artwork, no brand's. The fonts
are from Google Fonts, all under the SIL Open Font License: Kanit, Russo One (Rally2), Bungee, Racing Sans One (Rally4
Junior Cup), Righteous, Lobster (Group 4 Classics), Alfa Slab One (Sixties Heroes).

- `<key>.png`: the series on its coloured tile, 640 x 360 (the RANKED page's series rows, HOME's RANKED tile)
- `<key>-logo.png`: the logo alone, transparent, 600 x 340 (the session's and the results' headers)

The keys are the server's (server/src/liverallies.js SERIES): rally2, junior, classic, sixties.
